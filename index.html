The core message

The same workload — 48.92M inferences — packages into 489K credits on BAIC, or roughly 751B tokens on a frontier-model architecture. One credit replaces 100 inferences worth of variable token consumption.

Tokens price infrastructure consumption; credits price business outcomes. At default mid-size settings that's 84.4% lower customer cost. The deeper sell is risk transfer — model price changes, prompt verbosity, and efficiency variance all sit with Uniphore, not the customer.

Source: Uniphore Internal Credit Calculator · This page mirrors the calculator's math model · For internal training use only

Token model: "High token" inferences (long context, e.g. doc parsing, fine-tuning, user-facing RAG queries) = 23,000 tokens/inference avg. "Low token" inferences (structured KB lookups, agent runs, monitoring) = 2,800 tokens/inference avg. Token rate: $20 per 1M tokens (blended frontier, GPT-4o / Gemini Pro tier). Credit model: 1 credit = 100 inferences; $5.00 per credit. Calibrated against the calculator's $15.15M token cost reference at Mid-Size defaults.
